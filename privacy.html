Donjon Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Overview

Donjon is a macOS menu bar application that backs up folders on your Mac to external USB drives. Donjon does not require an account, collects no personal data, contains no analytics or tracking, and sends nothing over the network.

Your Data Stays on Your Devices

Backup plan configurations and backup history are stored locally on your Mac. Backup snapshots are written only to the external drives you choose. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Encryption Passphrases

If you enable encryption for a backup plan, your passphrase is stored in the macOS Keychain on your Mac and is used only to encrypt and decrypt your backups locally. It never leaves your machine.

File Access

Donjon runs in the macOS App Sandbox and can only read folders you explicitly grant access to (via folder pickers and drive access prompts). Access is used solely to create and restore your backups.

Purchases

The optional Donjon Pro upgrade is processed by Apple through the App Store. Donjon receives no personal or payment information from purchases.
